Kinetic equations
=================
This section describes the equation terms applied to the kinetic quantities
in DREAM, i.e. the hot-tail and runaway electron distribution functions
:math:`f_{\rm hot}(r,p,\xi)` and :math:`f_{\rm re}(r,p,\xi)`. These are
solved using a bounce-averaged drift-kinetic equation in
:math:`(r, p, \xi_0)` coordinates.
